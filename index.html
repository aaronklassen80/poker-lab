About Poker Lab v0.3

Multi-table tournament trainer. Starts with 36 players at six-handed tables. Simulates up to 14 late arrivals and up to 8 optional bot re-entries. Late registration closes after 14 rounds. New tables open, existing tables consolidate, and opponents move between tables as needed.

12,000 starting chips; 50/100 opening blinds, five hands per level, and a big-blind ante beginning at level three. This is an illustrative training structure, not the verified Running Aces schedule. Top seven places receive simulated 35/22/15/10/8/6/4% payouts.

Coach limitation: exact pot odds and Monte Carlo equity versus uniformly random hands do not establish the best play against an actual range. There is no validated GTO solver or ICM model yet. Opponents remain experimental heuristics.

Off-screen tables play one simulated hand per hand at your table. Game progress saves in this browser, not in a cloud account. No real-money features.
