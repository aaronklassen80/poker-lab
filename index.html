About Poker Lab v0.1

This first build is a six-player, no-rake, simulated-money cash game with legal action checks, all-ins, side pots, hand rankings, and basic computer opponents.

Coach: exact immediate pot odds and Monte Carlo showdown-equity estimates against uniformly random opponent cards. It does not yet know actual opponent betting ranges or provide solver-verified GTO advice.

Next: tested preflop ranges, stronger bot benchmarks, complete hand replay, tournament rules, and a persistent account system.

This is a learning prototype, not a real-money gambling application.
